How the ranking works

Every row is a protocol that records no token in the snapshot — no CoinGecko id and no ticker — and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not a centralised exchange, and not a versioned name. The board covers all 40 of the snapshot's watchlist protocols, then the early finds below the $50M floor.

Implied value = TVL × growth × age × breadth. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, worth −30%…+60%. Age is the years since DefiLlama first tracked the protocol with no token recorded, up to 5 years, worth up to +30%. Breadth is one chain at par, +3% per further chain up to six more. The value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth — never a prediction that a token is issued, or when.

Each row carries a 7-step farm plan with amounts, costs and time. A plan is a starting point, not advice: check the protocol's own docs before you move funds, and never share a seed phrase or sign a transaction you cannot read.
